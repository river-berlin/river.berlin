# Digging into Deutche Bahn data, what can I find?

Piebro has a Deutche Bahn dataset available on https://github.com/piebro/deutsche-bahn-data 

I have had many sneaking suspictions and questions with regard to Deutche Bahn - so I have gone digging digging digging

one note on this data - Deutsche Bahn absolutely lies about the data presented in the official API, this is especially true for buses. This is a very known phenomena, wherein your app will tell you that the bus has passed, even though it is late and hasn't come yet.

This has come with lived experience in Berlin, so just keep that in mind.

The memes of "Deutsche Bahn" being late are known, but how true are they?

just fyi : I did use Claude to help me out here, with some of the statistics, I did verify them however

Data Caveat - Due to limited storage in my macbook for downloading data, I've only analysed data from the last 9 months.
